---
title: Tracking domain
description: Put open, click, unsubscribe and "view in browser" links on your own domain.
---

Every marketing email carries links that go through SendLit: the open-tracking
pixel, tracked clicks, the unsubscribe link and the "view in browser" link. By
default they use SendLit's domain. With a tracking domain they use yours, for
example `track.example.com`:

- links match your brand and the domain in your From address;
- your links' reputation is your own, not shared with other senders.

Transactional emails with open or click tracking use it too.

## Set it up

1. In **Settings → General → Tracking domain**, enter a subdomain you own,
   such as `track.example.com`, and save. A root domain such as `example.com`
   can't be used, because it can't have a CNAME record.
2. At your DNS provider, add the CNAME record SendLit shows: the name is your
   subdomain and the value is SendLit's tracking host. On Cloudflare, set the
   record to **DNS only** (grey cloud).
3. SendLit checks the record and that it can answer over HTTPS on your domain.
   It rechecks every hour, or press **Check now**. The HTTPS certificate is
   usually ready a few minutes after DNS updates.

Once the domain shows **Verified**, new emails use it. Emails already sent keep
their links.

The API has the same settings: `GET`, `PUT /settings/tracking-domain` and
`POST /settings/tracking-domain/verify`, and the MCP tools
`get_tracking_domain`, `set_tracking_domain` and `verify_tracking_domain`.

## Keep the record in place

Links in emails you've sent point at your tracking domain for as long as
people open them, so keep the CNAME record. SendLit checks a verified domain
every day. If it fails three days in a row, it shows **Not working** and new
emails go back to SendLit's domain until it works again.

If you change or remove the domain, SendLit keeps answering on the old one, so
links in emails you've already sent keep working as long as its CNAME record
stays.

## Return-Path (bounce) domain

The tracking domain is only for links. The Return-Path (bounce, or MAIL FROM)
domain is set at your email provider, and SendLit checks it as part of
[domain authentication](/email-marketing/domain-authentication): its SPF record
is the one that counts for DMARC alignment.
