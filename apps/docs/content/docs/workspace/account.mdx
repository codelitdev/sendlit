The dashboard home shows the current team’s delivery activity and allowance: sent, queued, failed, bounced, active sequences, scheduled broadcasts, and daily/monthly quota usage.

Your account is your login and is never billed. Plans and subscriptions belong
to organizations, not accounts, logins, or teams. Open
[Organizations](/workspace/organizations) to view plan, payment status, limits,
and usage for a selected organization. See [Pricing and billing](/workspace/pricing)
for the complete plan rules.

Quotas shown on the dashboard are team-scoped, so switching teams changes the
numbers shown there. Organization billing usage is shown on the organization's
**Plan** tab.

## Delete your account

Open **Account** and choose **Delete account** in the danger zone. The
dialog lists every organization you belong to and what happens to it:

- **Will be deleted**: you are its only owner. It is deleted like
  [deleting an organization](/workspace/organizations), and the personal
  data it holds about its contacts is erased: contacts and their fields,
  events and enrolments, signup confirmations, recipient addresses in
  delivery history and transactional email, and raw ESP webhook payloads.
  Suppressed addresses stay blocked through an anonymous hash.
- **You'll leave**: another owner stays in charge. The organization keeps
  its contacts, campaigns, keys and billing; you only lose access.

Some things have to happen first, and the dialog links to where you fix
them:

- an organization you own alone has an active or paid-through
  subscription, or an open checkout: cancel it first;
- you own an organization alone and other people are members: make one of
  them an owner, or remove them;
- you pay for the subscription of an organization you would leave: ask an
  owner to cancel it or take over billing.

To confirm, type your email and enter the code SendLit emails you. Codes
expire after 5 minutes. When the account is deleted your name and email are
erased and you are signed out everywhere, including OAuth and MCP clients.
Billing records and audit events are kept for accounting and security, with
an anonymous id instead of your name or email. Signing up again with the
same email creates a new, empty account.

The REST API offers the same flow from a dashboard session only:
`GET /account/deletion`, `POST /account/deletion/code` and `POST /account/deletion`.
It isn't available over MCP or with API keys.
