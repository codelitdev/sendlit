## Export from beehiiv

1. In beehiiv, open **Settings** and select your publication.
2. Open **Export Data**.
3. Under **Export Subscribers**, choose **Export All Subscribers** (the full
   export, with custom fields) or **Export All Basic Subscribers** (email,
   status and tier only).
4. beehiiv emails you when the export is ready; you can also download it
   from **Historical Exports** on the same page. The download link is valid
   for 24 hours, so import it the same day or export again.

To keep your past issues, also choose **Export All Posts** under **Export
Posts**. SendLit can't import posts into the web archive yet, so keep that
file.

## Import into SendLit

In **Contacts → Import**, upload the CSV. SendLit recognises beehiiv's
columns: the email, the status and the `tier`, which becomes a tag such as
`tier:premium`. The full export's custom fields become custom fields; set any
column you don't need to "Don't import". Add a tag such as `beehiiv` to every
contact.

| beehiiv status | In SendLit |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Subscribed |
| Paused | Subscribed, tagged `paused` |
| Inactive | Unsubscribed |
| Pending, Needs Approval | Skipped, or unsubscribed and tagged `pending` if you tick the option |

Paused subscribers asked for a break, not to leave. Leave them out of
broadcasts with an audience filter that excludes the `paused` tag until
you're ready.

Premium subscribers keep their tier as a tag, so you can send them premium
issues. SendLit doesn't take payments; keep billing them through your
payment provider.
